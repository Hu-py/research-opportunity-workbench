# Method Transfer

1. Read the user's research profile and identify a real research bottleneck, available variables and candidate outcome metrics.
2. For each computational method, articulate its assumptions, inputs, expected outputs and computational cost.
3. Construct the transfer mapping: urban bottleneck -> method capability -> compatibility requirements -> baseline -> falsifiable experiment.
4. Propose a small experiment with success and failure criteria; do not conflate method adoption with scientific novelty.
5. If resources, data labels or experimental feasibility are unknown, mark them explicitly as assumptions.

Do not write a definitive “novel” finding without targeted prior-art checking. Prefer 1-3 well-supported directions over 10 speculative ideas.
