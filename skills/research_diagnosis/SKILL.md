# SC05 · Paper-only research diagnosis

## Interface
**Required input: one published or authorized paper.** The normal user uploads only a PDF; ask for no additional raw data or code at intake. Additional files are optional and may be proposed as future *feasibility requirements*, never prerequisites for generating the initial report. Support other publication topics without making the Zhengzhou or child-health projects implicit baselines.

**Output:** one readable Chinese research-diagnosis Markdown report, one source-and-model audit JSON, and a separated blind evidence snapshot. A published PDF may be analyzed for the requesting user; do not commit complete publisher text to GitHub. Explicitly distinguish model hypotheses from author findings, externally retrieved studies, and original numerical recomputation (not performed).

## Procedure (required order)
1. **Freeze the paper.** Read title, abstract, Introduction, Methods (including formulas/figures), Data, Results and *empirical* limitations. Preserve original pages and actual quoted excerpts, document and PDF hash. In first-pass blind tests withhold the author's Future Work/Discussion recommendations, previous assistant proposals and expected answer key, not meaningful negative results. Read PDF captions and diagram labels where extraction permits; mark unparsed equations/figures as unverified.
2. **Build a method/data dependency graph before proposing any method.** For each transformation record source data → observed/derived/simulated variable → formula, assumptions → evaluation → claim. Look explicitly for paths in which the proposed target is derived from candidate predictors, simulations are interpreted as observations, pre-event and post-event information are mixed, an outcome lacks independent evidence, or an intervention assumes fixed behavior. These are *diagnostic questions*, never automatic assertions of error.
3. **Two-track scientific assessment (mandatory).** First reconstruct the original research questions and evaluate design rigor **relative to the claims actually made**. A paper's scoped assumptions, noncausal associations, stylized simulations or omitted alternative evaluations are not automatically flaws. Log measurement and validation boundary checks in a *quality-review lane*, not as recommended follow-on studies. Independently extract **scientific frontiers** from positive findings, unresolved interactions, emergent phenomena, decision feedback, scale changes and mechanisms. Every frontier must cite an original observed fact, name an unanswered system mechanism, provide competing explanations, and connect an observation → tension → mechanism → scientific implication in a coherent next-paper narrative. Do not require every scientific question to come from a prior diagnostic shortcoming. For each proposed frontier explain why it is not just another sensitivity or validation test. Zero defensible frontiers is acceptable.
4. **Independent diagnosis.** Use only verified source fact IDs. Provide competing mechanisms and falsifiable checks. Separate actual result, author interpretation, hypothesis and unknown. Do not force a number of findings. Use the quality lane to improve interpretation, but only promote a science frontier into the next-paper pool.
5. **Plan targeted literature searches from scientific frontier questions (and relevant diagnoses).** Seek theory, methods, independent empirical cases, negative findings and nearest competitors. Use OpenAlex or trustworthy primary publisher/preprint metadata with DOI/URL, date, abstract and clearly reported evidence level. Explore beyond the monitored journal directory, but do not silently approve a new permanent source. Do not say a method was evaluated in the source if the abstract does not say so.
6. **Compare near papers before generating research routes.** Record each candidate's actual task, known methodology, overlap, assumptions needed to transfer, limitations and whether it is a contradiction/counterexample. Ground each abstract-derived assertion in a literal excerpt. A short methods table is worth more than ten generic paper links.
7. **Redesign research, not just models.** Allow changes in the model, data, validation design and research question; compare routes without requiring one per type. For each route specify what *new* mechanism/question is added, what original evidence cannot answer, directly closest research, data feasibility, explicit baseline, minimum experiment, failure criterion and an alternative if data are missing. Zero good routes is a valid result.
8. **Independent scientific critic.** Try to invalidate each proposed path; label test / hold / reject. No research opportunity is called novel or publishable before fuller near-neighbor examination and, where necessary, new data.
9. **Deliver complete report and audit.** See `references/report-quality-gates.md`. Preserve snapshot SHA, source page IDs, citations, used external paper IDs, model stage/token usage, unresolved limitations and failed searches. If no real LLM call was made, label the output an offline workflow demonstration, not a SC05 blind experimental result.

## Explicitly prohibited shortcuts
- Do not ask for source code/OD/raw records merely to **understand** published formulas. Request minimal validation data only at the point of proposing a funded/reproducible experiment.
- Never put a previous human-authored research-direction report into the initial blind diagnosis prompt or pretend an assistant-generated draft was discovered autonomously.
- Do not give identical-looking methods as three different ideas, paste a generic "add GNN" recommendation, or claim that changing the study region alone is necessarily novel.
- **Do not turn methodological quality assurance into a new paper proposal.** Examples: uncertainty checks, formula sensitivities, alternate regressions and independent validation may support a new scientific question, but cannot substitute for one. Evaluate original design fairness first, and let a new research opportunity stand on an independent scientific problem.
- Do not claim model performance, theoretical guarantees, deployment effectiveness, causal evidence or novelty beyond what the source and separately verified literature establish.
- Never reproduce full proprietary paper content in the report. Use short source excerpts and page anchors; do not print key values or server-only prompts to logs.
- A failed literature search is a validation gap, not proof that the topic is unexplored.
