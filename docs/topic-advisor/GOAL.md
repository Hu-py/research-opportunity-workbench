# Research Topic Advisor

Reduce the human discussion cost of selecting scientifically valuable, falsifiable and feasible urban science / computing research topics. Produce at most three recommendations, including an explicit no-recommendation outcome. Neither LLM agreement nor automated scoring certifies novelty or publishability.

Scope: evidence/context, confirmed decision memory, three discovery paths, scientific criticism, revision, portfolio and feedback. Excludes executing scientific studies, writing papers and research-reward reinforcement learning.
